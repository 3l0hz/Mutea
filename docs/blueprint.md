# **App Name**: PawSense

## Core Features:

- Premium Product Catalog: Browse and filter a curated selection of smart pet toys, gadgets, and accessories with high-quality images and clear pricing. Includes categorized sections for easy navigation.
- Interactive Product Details: View detailed information, multiple emotive images, and feature descriptions for each product, with direct options to add to cart or inquire.
- AI-Powered Discovery Tool: Receives personalized product recommendations based on browsing history, pet preferences, and popular trends to enhance product discovery.
- Streamlined Mobile Checkout: A user-friendly, multi-step checkout process optimized for mobile devices, supporting secure payments and shipping information input.
- WhatsApp Customer Support Integration: A direct button on product pages and in the footer to consult specific product details or order inquiries via WhatsApp.
- Differentiated Value Proposition Section: Dedicated section on the homepage highlighting 'Why Buy Here', emphasizing unique products, gifting potential, quick purchases, local shipping in Chile, and personalized WhatsApp attention.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: A sophisticated, muted olive green (#8AA145) to convey warmth and a touch of modern tech, reflecting innovation and natural comfort for pets.
- Background color: A very light, warm green-beige (#F4F5F0) that provides a clean, calming, and upscale canvas for product displays.
- Accent color: A subtle, warm terracotta orange (#DB9D70) for call-to-action buttons and subtle highlights, drawing attention without being overpowering, as a discreet accent.
- Body and headline font: 'PT Sans' (sans-serif) for its modern yet warm personality, ensuring readability on mobile and aligning with a clean, accessible premium aesthetic.
- Clean, modern, and friendly line-art icons that complement the premium app-like design, avoiding excessive detail or cartoonish styles.
- Mobile-first layout with generous visual separation and modular sections (rounded-3xl blocks) that feel like distinct app components, using soft shadows and large, emotional product images to guide the user's eye.
- Subtle, fast, and functional animations focused on user feedback and smooth transitions (e.g., adding to cart, loading states), avoiding flashy or excessive motion to maintain a 'premium accesible' and performant feel.
